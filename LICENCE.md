## 🧩 About

**Counter-Strike 2 Overlay** is a comprehensive overlay for Counter-Strike 2 featuring ESP, radar, bomb timer, spectator list, performance stats, and custom crosshair.

Based on popular overlays like **OSCAR**, **CS2 Internal**, and **GameOverlay**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Features
- ESP — see enemies through walls
- Radar — show enemy positions on radar
- Bomb Timer — display bomb countdown
- Spectator List — show who is spectating
- Performance Stats — FPS, ping, tick rate

### 🎨 Customization
- Custom Crosshair — design your own crosshair
- Theme Editor — change overlay colors
- Font Selection — choose overlay font

### 🏋️ Utility
- Autoexec Config — load custom settings
- Quick Switch — fast weapon switching
- Demo Recording — record and replay matches
